## The story so far

Before Haas, I was Chief of Staff at Rocket Learning, an ed-tech startup backed by
ex-McKinsey and HBS founders, where I led AI transformation strategy, built
partnerships with Google and OpenAI, and took a product from 0 to 1 that reached
2M users.

Before that, I spent 2.5 years at Samagra, India's leading governance consulting
firm, solving large-scale public systems problems using data.

At Samagra, I built a reading app with Google for Education that reached 15M+
learners.
